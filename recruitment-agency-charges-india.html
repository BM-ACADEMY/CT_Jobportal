What Does a Recruitment Agency Do?

A recruitment agency acts as an intermediary connecting businesses that have open vacancies with qualified candidates seeking employment. Professional recruitment agencies add value across several operational stages:

  • Talent Sourcing: Searching active job boards, talent pools, social channels, and proprietary candidate databases.
  • Screening & Assessment: Conducting resume validation, initial screening calls, technical tests, and preliminary interviews.
  • Interview Coordination: Managing schedules between hiring managers and shortlisted applicants.
  • Salary Negotiation & Onboarding: Assisting both parties in reaching mutual agreement on compensation, notice period buyout, and joining dates.

What Recruitment Agencies Actually Charge in India

Recruitment agency pricing in India is not arbitrary. It follows structured commercial models determined by seniority, hiring volume, and employment nature:

1. Percentage-Based Recruitment Fee

Percentage fees are linked directly to the hired candidate's annual Cost to Company (CTC).

The percentage-based contingency model is the most widely adopted recruitment fee structure in India for permanent full-time positions. Under this model, the agency charges an agreed percentage of the candidate's first-year annual Cost to Company (CTC). Standard market rates generally fall into these tiers:

  • Junior & Entry-Level Roles (up to ₹4 LPA): 8.33% (equivalent to approximately 1 month's gross salary) or a negotiated flat fee.
  • Mid-Level Roles (₹4 LPA to ₹15 LPA): 8.33% to 12.5% of annual CTC depending on skill scarcity and hiring volume.
  • Senior & Leadership Positions (₹15 LPA to ₹30+ LPA): 12.5% to 18%+ of annual CTC.
  • Key rule: This fee is strictly paid by the employer after the candidate successfully joins the company.

2. Fixed-Fee Recruitment

In fixed-fee recruitment, the employer and agency agree on a predetermined flat fee per successful placement regardless of the candidate's exact final salary. For example, a business hiring 5 sales executives in Pondicherry might agree on a fixed fee of ₹15,000 per hire. This model provides budget predictability for companies hiring in high volume.

3. Retainer or Project-Based Recruitment

Retained search firms dedicate full-time headhunters to confidential and specialized C-level roles.

Retained recruitment is used for mission-critical executive appointments (such as VP of Engineering, Chief Financial Officer, or Plant Head). The employer pays an upfront retainer fee in stages:

  • Milestone 1: 33% on contract initiation and candidate specification setup.
  • Milestone 2: 33% upon delivery of the qualified candidate shortlist.
  • Milestone 3: Remaining balance upon candidate acceptance and joining.

4. Contract Staffing and Manpower Charges

Contract staffing encompasses candidate salary, statutory compliance, and a transparent agency markup.

For temporary, seasonal, or project-based employment, the agency employs the staff on its own payroll and deploys them to the client's premises. The commercial structure comprises:

  • The candidate's monthly gross take-home pay.
  • Mandatory statutory compliance contributions (EPF, ESI, Gratuity, Bonus, and Labor Welfare Fund).
  • An agency management markup or fee (typically 10% to 20% of CTC, or a fixed monthly administrative fee per deployed employee).

Do Job Seekers Have to Pay Recruitment Agencies?

Indian labor guidelines mandate that domestic recruitment fees are the sole responsibility of the hiring employer.

In standard domestic recruitment in India, job seekers do NOT have to pay recruitment agencies. Legitimate recruitment consultancies earn their revenue entirely from the hiring company. Any agency or individual that demands money from a candidate for job alerts, registration, interview scheduling, or appointment letter release is operating unethically or committing fraud.

  • Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) Overseas Exception: For overseas employment, government-registered recruiting agents (RA) are legally permitted to charge a strictly capped service fee (up to ₹30,000 + GST as per MEA guidelines) with an official tax invoice.
  • Domestic Jobs: For jobs within Tamil Nadu, Pondicherry, and India, candidate fees are entirely prohibited.

What Should Employers Check Before Hiring a Recruitment Agency?

Before signing a Master Service Agreement (MSA) with a recruitment firm, employers should evaluate four key commercial elements:

Key Commercial Elements to Review:

  1. Understand the Fee Structure: Clarify whether the percentage is calculated on Basic Salary, Fixed CTC, or Total CTC including variable bonuses.
  2. Check the Replacement Guarantee Policy: If a hired candidate resigns or is terminated within 30, 60, or 90 days, does the agency provide a free replacement or credit note?
  3. Understand the Payment Trigger: Confirm whether payment is due 15, 30, or 45 days after the candidate's date of joining.
  4. Confirm the Sourcing Channels: Ensure the agency uses verified methods and does not misrepresent your employer brand.

What Should Job Seekers Check?

If contacted by a recruitment agency claiming to represent an employer, candidates should always verify the following parameters:

  • Employer Name: Ask for the specific corporate entity hiring for the role.
  • Job Role & JD: Request a formal, detailed Job Description with clear responsibilities.
  • Work Location: Confirm the actual work site and shift timings.
  • Salary & Benefits: Verify that the compensation package matches market benchmarks.
  • Selection Process: Legitimate agencies will arrange genuine interviews with the client company.
  • Payment Request: If any fee is requested, decline immediately and report the contact.

How to Compare Recruitment Agencies

When selecting a recruitment partner, businesses should not choose solely on the lowest percentage fee. Consider these four critical comparative factors:

  • Domain Specialization: Does the agency specialize in your sector (e.g., IT, healthcare, manufacturing, or retail)?
  • Local Geographic Reach: Do they possess strong candidate networks in Pondicherry, Villupuram, Cuddalore, and surrounding industrial belts?
  • Screening Depth: Do they submit pre-vetted resumes or simply forward unverified keyword-matched files?
  • Turnaround Time (TAT): How quickly can they provide a qualified shortlist (typically 3 to 7 business days)?

Questions Employers Should Ask Before Signing

Ask these questions during agency negotiations:

  • What is your fee percentage, and is it calculated on Fixed pay or Total CTC?
  • What is the duration of your replacement guarantee (30, 60, or 90 days)?
  • What happens if a candidate leaves within the replacement window and you cannot provide a suitable replacement?
  • What are your payment credit terms (15, 30, or 45 days)?

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What recruitment agencies actually charge in India?

Standard recruitment agencies in India charge employers between 8.33% and 15%+ of the candidate's first-year annual CTC for permanent staffing, or a monthly management markup for contract staffing.

2. Who usually pays a recruitment agency?

The employer always pays the recruitment agency. Professional recruitment agencies never charge candidates for job placements in India.

3. Do recruitment agencies charge candidates for jobs?

No. Legitimate domestic agencies never charge candidates. Any agency demanding registration fees, interview fees, or placement charges from job seekers is engaging in unethical practices.

4. How can an employer compare recruitment agency charges?

Compare the fee percentage, calculation base (Fixed vs Total CTC), replacement guarantee period, payment terms, and candidate screening quality rather than cost alone.
